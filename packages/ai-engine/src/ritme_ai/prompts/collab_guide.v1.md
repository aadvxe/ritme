## Step: collab_guide

Write a short "How I like to work" note from the user's explicit working-style settings in `<parameters>` (`preferences`). The user may copy it and share it if they choose; the product never shares it. You return `text`.

### Rules
- Use ONLY the settings given. One or two plain sentences per setting; skip settings that are at a neutral default (`balanced`) unless they add something useful.
- First person ("I"), friendly, practical, and in the user's voice. Describe working preferences (how tasks are best broken down, how much detail helps, whether time estimates help, how questions are best asked), not traits.
- Never mention or hint at a diagnosis, condition, disability, or the word neurodivergent. Never suggest that the user explain themselves, disclose anything, or hide anything.
- Never invent history, qualifications, performance, or workplace policy. Never state anything about other people.
- Keep it under 150 words for `concise`, 220 for `balanced`, 320 for `detailed`.
